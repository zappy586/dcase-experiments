Listening test

For each machine: listen to the 5 normal references, then mark each of the 10 test clips as normal or anomaly (5 of each, shuffled). All clips are source domain, near mic, original level. Press Check when all 10 are marked.

Results so far

Guessing gets 5 of 10 on average; 8 or more of 10 on one machine happens by luck about 5–10% of the time; 10 of 10 well under 1%.
